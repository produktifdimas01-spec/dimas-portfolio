About Me

Analytical Mindset.
Leadership Backbone.

I am a fresh graduate in Data Science with hands-on experience across government statistics, academic laboratory environments, and organizational leadership. My academic journey equipped me with strong quantitative foundations, while my practical experiences sharpened my ability to communicate data insights in professional and decision-making contexts.

As a former intern at Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS), Indonesia's national statistics body, I was exposed to large-scale data workflows, government reporting structures, and the discipline required in public-sector data governance. Concurrently, my dual roles as a Deep Learning and Spatial Data Analysis Lab Assistant strengthened my technical depth and ability to mentor peers effectively.

Beyond technical work, I have consistently taken on leadership responsibilities within student organizations — building collaboration frameworks, managing cross-functional teams, and driving initiatives from planning to execution. I am now eager to bring this combination of analytical capability and leadership readiness into a high-impact corporate or BI environment.
